Kilwa fishers vote to extend octopus closure

By Amina Mbwana

Fishers in three villages south of Kilwa have voted to keep their reef flats closed for a further three months, after the last closed season ended with catches twice the size of the year before.

The beach management unit said the decision was unanimous. Small-scale fishers who rely on the flats will be offered places on a seaweed farming scheme while the closure lasts.
